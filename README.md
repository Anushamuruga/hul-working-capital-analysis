# HUL Working Capital Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a study on the impact of Working Capital Management on the profitability of Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL).

The analysis focuses on working capital management, profitability, financial performance, and financial ratios using financial data of HUL.

## 🎯 Objectives

- To study the working capital management of HUL.
- To analyse the profitability of HUL.
- To evaluate the impact of working capital management on profitability.
- To analyse the financial performance of HUL.
- To interpret financial ratios and understand the overall financial position of the company.

## 📅 Period of Analysis

FY 2021 – FY 2025

## 🔍 Key Areas of Analysis

- Working Capital Management
- Profitability Analysis
- Liquidity Analysis
- Financial Ratio Analysis
- Financial Performance Analysis
